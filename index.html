Tae-Gon Noh

M.S. candidate in Neuroscience, Seoul National University

Seoul National University

I am an M.S. candidate in Neuroscience at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Ki-Young Jung. My research uses machine learning, sleep physiology, and clinical neuroscience to model disease progression in isolated REM sleep behavior disorder and related alpha-synucleinopathies.

I am preparing for Ph.D. training in neuroscience and medical AI. I am especially interested in building multimodal biomarkers from PSG, EEG, EMG, brain MRI, and clinical data that can improve early diagnosis, staging, and prognosis in neurodegenerative disease.

Google Scholar / ORCID / LinkedIn / GitHub

Research focus

  • Foundation model-based medical AI for disease progression modeling in Parkinson’s disease and isolated REM sleep behavior disorder.
  • Quantitative sleep and electrophysiology biomarkers, including REM sleep without atonia, EMG amplitude, EEG connectivity, phase-amplitude coupling, ERPs, and eye-blinking behavior.
  • Clinical prediction, survival modeling, heterogeneity modeling, and longitudinal analysis for prodromal neurodegenerative disease.
